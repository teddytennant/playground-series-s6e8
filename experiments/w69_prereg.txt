3. THE INSTRUMENT (w69a_factorial.py) -- A 2x2, NOT A LADDER
================================================================================
ONE process, ONE load of the 211 pack, FOUR arms formed by deleting columns, every arm on
the SAME fold partition for a given seed. Factors: A = the three ext_members14 (lexb)
members present/absent; B = the nine ext_members15 (`yadoy666`) streams present/absent.

    A199 = -A -B      A202 = +A -B      A208 = -A +B      A211 = +A +B

The estimands, every one of them exactly paired because the shared arm and most of the
partition term cancel (w65 section 0's lesson: FORM THE CONTRAST, do not compare derived
means):

    E_A_lo  = A202 - A199      lexb's effect WITHOUT the nine   [replicates 1(a)]
    E_A_hi  = A211 - A208      lexb's effect WITH the nine
    E_B_lo  = A208 - A199      the nine's effect WITHOUT lexb   [ARM 208's matched control]
    E_B_hi  = A211 - A202      the nine's effect WITH lexb      [re-derives 1(b) in-process]
    INTER   = E_A_hi - E_A_lo  = E_B_hi - E_B_lo                [the new quantity]

A ladder of pack sizes cannot separate INTER from the two main effects; only the 2x2 can,
and the missing cell (208) is the one nobody has built. INTER is the candidate mechanism
w68 section 7 item 4 names for "ARM 211 reads BELOW 199 on every stored base".

Seeds 42,101,13,7 -- w65a's, unchanged, so E_A_lo is a like-for-like replication and not a
new draw. Per-seed results are written to disk as they complete so a partial run is
readable by a later run; w65a's all-or-nothing artefact cost three runs of waiting.
