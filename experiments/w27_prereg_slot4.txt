CONTEXT / WHY THIS AND NOT THE HANDED ANGLE AS WRITTEN
=====================================================
Handed angle: "XGBoost: third leg of the ensemble, tuned on the same folds so the
blend weights mean something."

The literal reading is closed by this workspace's own measurements and re-running it
would be filler:
  - The pack already holds ~40 XGBoost members across FOUR distinct upstream
    pipelines (run_xgb.py modes lat / cat / raw / latcat), all on the frozen
    SKF5 seed42 folds, so the blend weights already mean something.
  - GBDT hyperparameter tuning inside a fixed pipeline is closed at ~+4e-7 into
    the stack (RESEARCH, 2026-08-13).
  - w27l ranked decorrelation over all 188 members: the lat-pipeline XGB members
    are the MOST redundant objects we own (xgb_lat 176/188, xgb_latcat 181/188).
  - Slot 7 of 2026-08-16 (w16q/w16r) is the literal angle, already executed.

What IS open in the XGBoost direction, and is the load-bearing weakness in the
strongest live thread: w27j's cross-class CT scorecard (yesterday's SS10) rests on
FOLD 0 ONLY, n=1, for both xgb and cat, and its LightGBM control is QUOTED from a
different script (w27g) rather than refitted on the same harness. Three headline
claims were written off that single cell:
    "the 4/3 skew is a property of the feature matrix, not a LightGBM artefact"
    "CatBoost leans on CT_ 1.6x harder than LightGBM"
    "the TE re-shrink is negative outside LightGBM"
w26k already shows the per-fold spread of d_c is large (LGB @2000 rounds:
+695 / +519 / +497e-6 on folds 0/1/2 -- a range of ~200e-6, ~35% of the mean), so
a 34e-6 xgb-vs-lgb gap read off one fold is almost certainly unresolvable.

w27j costs 57s (xgb) / 37s (cat) per fit at 400 rounds. All five folds are cached.
The full 3-class x 5-fold grid is 13 remaining fits, ~15 min of 3-thread work. This
is the cheapest available upgrade of an n=1 headline to n=5, it puts the XGBoost leg
on the same folds as everything else it is compared to, and it costs no submission.
