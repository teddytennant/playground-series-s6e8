3. GATE A -- es-on-val, per member. The clause's own question.
================================================================================
The artefact: the author chooses the stopping point using the SAME rows whose predictions
they then publish as out-of-fold. That inflates the published OOF and does not transfer to
test, so a stacker fitted on our frozen folds over-weights the column.

Verdict per member, decided ONLY on what the author's own log/source shows:

  PASS  -- no early stopping at all (a fixed n_estimators / max_epochs is reached on
           every fold), OR early stopping performed on an INNER split that provably
           excludes the rows published as that fold's OOF.
  FAIL  -- the stopping point is selected on the scored fold. Signatures, any one of
           which is sufficient: `eval_set`/`validation_data`/`eval_metric` carrying the
           held-out fold together with `early_stopping_rounds`, `use_best_model=True`,
           CatBoost `od_type`/`od_wait`, a Keras/Torch `EarlyStopping` callback, or log
           lines of the form "best iteration", "Restoring best model", "New best epoch".
  UNREADABLE -- the log does not show the fit loop and the source is not on disk.

⚠ UNREADABLE IS NOT PASS. w42b says the arm does not get the benefit of the doubt, and an
absence of evidence is exactly the state the clause was written to refuse to reward.

combiner scores on. RESEARCH already flags this shape for `sixmember_*` ("those are
level-2 stack outputs and the author discloses..."), so the precedent is ours, not new.

  PASS  -- the member is a level-1 model, OR a level-2 object whose own OOF is provably
           nested (the author states, or the log shows, an outer/inner split).
  FAIL  -- a level-2 stack or multi-model fusion whose published OOF is fitted on the
           rows it publishes.
  UNREADABLE -- as Gate A, and it counts as FAIL for promotion purposes.
