WHAT IS ACTUALLY OPEN, AND WHY IT IS STILL THE HANDED ANGLE
  The blend weights ARE searched on OOF here -- by an L2 logistic combiner. The one knob
  of that search never measured on the pack that actually ships is its PENALTY, and
  RESEARCH says so in as many words:

      "L2 on unstandardised columns is not a neutral prior. Hybrid column sds run
       1.81-27.58, so the widest member is shrunk ~230x less than the narrowest, purely
       from where the transform happens to put its logits. `--standardize` makes it
       isotropic. BUILT, NOT YET MEASURED; low prior given shrinkage itself is a null."

  Two facts make that stale rather than merely low-prior:
    1. EVERY current top build is standardised AND at C=1.0. `w27h_run.sh` ships
       `--standardize` with no `--lam`, so the penalty per row is 1/(1*553,095) = 1.8e-6
       against a log-loss of order 0.5. The isotropic prior was switched on and then left
       completely unpenalised. The two halves of the idea have never been run together.
    2. The one POSITIVE reading in the whole C literature here is unstandardised:
       C=0.01 is +5e-6 +/- 3e-6 consistent, and C=0.03 is +7e-6 on a separate 149-member
       sweep -- six positive measurements, two sweeps, two member sets, all ~5e-6, and
       RESEARCH's own summary line is "if anything set C=0.01". IT WAS NEVER SHIPPED.
    3. `ridge_sweep.py`'s own docstring states the transfer rule: standardising rescales
       the columns by 1/s, so the coefficients scale by ~s and ||w||^2 by ~s^2, and the
       optimal C moves DOWN "roughly by the square of" the scale. With median s of order
       5-8 on hybrid that is a 25-60x shift. So C=0.01 measured unstandardised does NOT
       name the standardised optimum, and C=1.0 -- what ships -- is further from it than
       the unstandardised fit ever was.
    4. RESEARCH's own binding rule: "Whenever n or the member count changes, recompute
       1/(C*n) before believing a C sweep." The pack has gone 156 -> 188 members since the
       sweep. n/p has fallen 4,400 -> 3,677.
