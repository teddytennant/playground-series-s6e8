2. THE INSTRUMENT (w65a_armpair.py)
================================================================================
w29d_partition.py's design, moved up eight members. ARM 202 is ARM 199 plus EXACTLY the three
`zhukovoleksiy` members in data/ext_members14 (lexb_cat_base, lexb_lgb02, lexb_xgb_base) --
w38d_run.sh is w36b_run.sh with `ext_members14` appended and nothing else changed. So ONE LOAD
SERVES BOTH ARMS: the 199 arm is the 202 matrix with those three columns deleted. Every
transform in agent/stack.py is strictly per-column and --standardize divides by a per-column
std, so subsetting after transform+scale is exactly equal to loading 199 members. That claim
is not assumed, it is GATED (P2).

Four arms, ONE process, IDENTICAL partitions, seeds 42,101,13,7:
    202          the full pack
    199          202 minus the three lexb columns                     <- the treatment
    PLA1, PLA2   202 minus three OTHER columns, drawn with np.random.default_rng(65)
                 from the 199 non-lexb members, disjoint from each other  <- MATCHED CONTROL
The placebo is matched by construction: it is the SAME operator (delete three columns from the
same scaled matrix, refit on the same partition) applied to columns that are not under test.
It is the "misaligned control" pattern RESEARCH.md recommends -- nothing is refitted with a
different operator, resampled or shuffled; only which three columns go.

Transforms: hybrid, rankraw, rescale, and h3 = their rank-average. h3 is the base the WANTED
files are built on. The WANTED objects are the `stdcorr` layer on top of h3; the 190, 194 and
195 promotions were all argued on the h3 base contrast and this one is too.

3. THE DECISION RULES, FIXED HERE
================================================================================
R1 (THE MOVE). `check_selection.WANTED` slot 1 moves from `w36_ad199stdcorr` to
    `w38_ad202stdcorr` IF AND ONLY IF BOTH:
       (i)  the paired h3 delta (202 - 199) is STRICTLY POSITIVE in >= 3 of the 4 partitions,
       AND
       (ii) its mean over the 4 partitions is >= +2.0e-6.
    Otherwise slot 1 STAYS. Referenced below as R1; no clause below re-states it in prose
    (w64 section 4: a decision rule must name its condition BY REFERENCE).
    Why >= +2.0e-6 and not the 4e-6 rebuild floor: the paired design cancels the BLAS-thread
    term exactly (w27z: A-B is 0.000e+00 to the last digit) but not the partition term, so the
    floor that applies here is the partition sd, not the cross-process one. 2.0e-6 is half the
    cross-process floor and ~1.5x w29d's measured per-partition sd of the same contrast.

R2 (THE PLACEBO). The three lexb members are declared NOT DISTINGUISHABLE FROM DELETING ANY
    THREE COLUMNS iff the lexb h3 delta mean lies INSIDE the closed interval spanned by the
    two placebo h3 delta means. R2 is REPORTED and moves NOTHING on its own; it is the
    calibration of R1's scale, not a second decision.
