⚠ WHY THE RULE IS AND-ED AND NOT OR-ED. The two instruments answer different questions and each

registered READING gives a run a motive to register only safe predictions. `w64a` writes its

decision is exactly the thing that should be conditional on the readings.

interest that DOES exist is the standing three-run deferral, and a deferral is a reason to
