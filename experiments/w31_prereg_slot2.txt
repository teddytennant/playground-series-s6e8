N0. WHY THIS AND NOT ANOTHER SWEEP
-----------------------------------
The handed angle is "LightGBM: tune it properly against the fixed folds -- learning rate,
leaves, regularisation, categorical handling", for the THIRD time (w26 slot 4, w27 slot 2,
now). It was refused twice as a priced null (day-1: +500e-6 on fold 0 -> +30e-6 on full OOF)
and taken the second time as the CT-skew INTERACTION question, registered at §K.

**§K finished, and it fired its own escape clause.** From w27g_tunect.log, fold 0, 400 rounds:

    K2(a)  d_c > 0 for all 14 configs                                        PASS
    K2(b)  spearman(CT_ gain share, d_c) = +0.464 (p=0.095, n=14)            PASS (modal +0.5)
    K2(c)  argmax @1.0 = lr0.05   argmax @4/3 = leaves255_dinf   DIFFER      PASS
    K2(d)  HEADLINE  best(4/3) - best(1.0) - 293.86e-6 = +146.75e-6          registered 0..+80

R-K3, verbatim: "If the headline clears +80e-6, the follow-on is a 5-fold re-run of that one
config against the control, not a member export." +146.75 clears +80. This slot is that
re-run. It is not a new sweep; the search is over and the grid is frozen at three configs.

N0b. THE CONFOUND §K DID NOT CONTROL, AND WHY IT IS THE HEADLINE HERE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Every number in the §K table was fitted at **400 rounds**. The control config IS
`lgbm_fixed_lat`, whose shipped member is fitted at **2000 rounds** for a stored CV of
**0.9677108350**. The 5-fold pooled control at 400 rounds (w26l_r400) is **0.9654813306**.

    rounds 400 -> 2000 is worth +2229.5e-6.

That is 1.5x the entire fold-0 spread of the 14-config table and 15x its headline. At fixed
rounds, `num_leaves`, `learning_rate` and `min_child_samples` are not only capacity knobs --
they are **speed-of-convergence knobs**, and a table read 2230e-6 below its own operating
point rewards whichever config gets there fastest, not whichever ends up highest. The two
fold-0 argmaxes are exactly the two configs that would be predicted on that reading alone:
`lr0.05` (2x the step) and `leaves255_dinf` (4x the leaves).

So the §K follow-on is run **at the operating point, 2000 rounds**, and staged so the rounds
axis is measured rather than assumed. This is free: boosting is sequential, so one 2000-round
fit yields every earlier round count via `num_iteration=`, exactly as §G3's serve-side
equivalence yields both CT arms off one booster. Two free axes, one fit.

N1. DESIGN
-----------
5 folds (the frozen StratifiedKFold(5, shuffle, seed 42)), 3 configs, 2000 rounds, seed 42,
the cached 184-column lattice matrices -- the same cache, folds and seed as w26l/w27g.

    configs   control (= lgbm_fixed_lat)
              leaves255_dinf   (num_leaves=255, max_depth=-1)   <- the R-K3 config
              lr0.05           (learning_rate=0.05)             <- fold-0 argmax @ s=1.0
    stages    400, 800, 1400, 2000        scored off one booster via num_iteration
    scales    s = 1.0 (status quo), s = 4/3 (the registered CT correction)

15 fits. Every cell is scored on identical rows, identical folds, identical trees; the only
things varying are the three registered axes. Pooled OOF over all 691,369 rows is the ONLY
currency (R-N1). Boosters and full OOF/test prediction cubes are checkpointed per (fold,
config), so every later stage/scale question is free from here.

N2. REGISTERED PRIORS, written before any fit
----------------------------------------------
(a) HARNESS GATE 1, free and exact. The first 400 trees of a 2000-round fit are the same
    trees a 400-round fit produces. So control @ stage 400 must reproduce w26l_r400's pooled
    numbers: **s=1.0 -> 0.9654813306, s=4/3 -> 0.9657751945**. Registered |delta| < 20e-6,
    expectation exact. A miss means the bagging RNG stream depends on n_estimators, which
    would invalidate the whole staged design and must be reported as such.
(b) HARNESS GATE 2, free and exact. control @ stage 2000 @ s=1.0 must reproduce
    `oof/summary_lgbm_fixed_lat.json`'s **0.9677108350**. Registered |delta| < 20e-6.
    Caveat carried from w26k: LightGBM's histogram reduction is not guaranteed bit-identical
    across thread counts; w27g nevertheless reproduced w26l's fold-0 control to 6 decimals at
    a different thread count, so exact is the expectation, not the hope.
(c) **THE HEADLINE.**  d_2000 = [leaves255_dinf @4/3 @2000] - [control @4/3 @2000], pooled.
    At 400 rounds on fold 0 this gap was **+1466.40e-6**. Registered: it SHRINKS BY AT LEAST
    80%, i.e. **point +150e-6, range -100 to +500e-6**, and P(leaves255_dinf still ahead at
    2000 rounds) = **0.6**. If most of the fold-0 gap survives to 2000 rounds, N0b is wrong
    and the workspace has been shipping an under-capacity LightGBM for three weeks.
(d) The §K headline, recomputed at the operating point:
    best(4/3)@2000 - best(1.0)@2000 - [the 2000-round control's own d_c].  Registered
    **0 to +80e-6, modal +15e-6** -- the SAME band §K registered, because the argument for it
    (day-1's base rate) was never about round count.
(e) d_c, the CT correction, on the control: +293.86e-6 at 400 rounds (w26l, pooled). At 2000
    rounds registered **still positive, 0.5x to 1.5x of it**. Direction of the rounds trend:
    registered INCREASING -- more trees put more thresholds on the CT_ block, so more of them
    are displaced by the same 33%. A decreasing d_c is the more interesting outcome and would
    say the correction's value is largest exactly where the shipped member is not.
(f) K2(c) at the operating point: registered that the argmax under s=4/3 is STILL at least as
    much capacity as the argmax under s=1.0. P(the two argmaxes differ at 2000 rounds) = 0.35
    -- lower than §K's 0.4, because the convergence confound that manufactured the fold-0
    split is removed.

N3. WHAT WOULD FALSIFY WHAT
----------------------------
  - gate (a) or (b) misses by >20e-6      -> the staged design is invalid; report and STOP.
                                             No config claim may be made off a failed harness.
  - d_2000 <= 0                           -> §K's table was an undertraining artefact, full
                                             stop, and R-K3's config does not exist. That is a
                                             clean result and closes the LightGBM angle for
                                             good, on evidence rather than on a priced null.
  - d_2000 keeps >=20% of +1466e-6        -> N0b is WRONG. The shipped lattice LightGBM is
                                             under-capacity and every member built on the
                                             control config is leaving >1000e-6 on the table.
  - d_c <= 0 at 2000 rounds               -> against §G's mechanism at the operating point,
                                             which is where it has never been tested.
