w96b PRE-REGISTERED DECISION RULE — written 2026-08-27, BEFORE any arm of w96a existed
======================================================================================

State of knowledge at the moment this file was written (verifiable: `logs_w96a_teprior_folds.txt`
held zero "AUC" lines and `experiments/w96a_teprior_folds.json` held no `folds` entry with an
arm in it; the run had been launched ~3 minutes earlier and was still building the fold-0
control frame):

  * w94a  12-column TE-only frame, fold 0, cheap preset : windowed - global = +520.313e-6
  * w94c  real 184-column frame, fold 0, cheap preset   : windowed - global = +123.872e-6
  * w96a  real 184-column frame, SHIPPED preset, 5 folds, with a null arm : RUNNING, NO ARMS YET
  * The selection margin the whole account rests on is +2.417e-6 of CV.
  * Nothing anywhere has ever measured this at the shipped preset, and nothing has ever
    measured the noise floor of a paired difference at that preset.

THE QUESTION THIS RULE DECIDES
------------------------------
One thing only: **do we spend the compute on a full windowed-prior OOF build** — a
`cache_win/` sibling of `cache/` plus one member trained off it, both on the frozen folds?

It does NOT decide whether that member enters the blend. That is a separate decision, made
later, on the member's OOF CV against the incumbents, under the existing member gates
(sd_test/sd_oof in [0.95, 1.45], the provenance rule, w60d, the value screen). A build that
happens and then loses on OOF CV is a build that told us the truth cheaply.

THE RULE
--------
Read `experiments/w96a_teprior_folds.json` after all five folds are present. Let

    S = [signal_e6] over the 5 folds   (windowed - global, same seed, same rows, same params)
    N = [null_e6]   over the 5 folds   (global_seed43 - global, IDENTICAL FRAME, seed alone)

BUILD if all three hold:

  A. SEPARATION.  mean(S) - mean(N) > 2 * sqrt( var(S)/5 + var(N)/5 )
     i.e. the signal clears the noise arm by two standard errors of their difference.
  B. CONSISTENCY. at least 4 of the 5 folds have signal_e6 > 0.
  C. MAGNITUDE.   mean(S) >= 25e-6 at the member level.
     Rationale, stated in advance so it cannot be revised downward afterwards: a member-level
     edge is diluted by every other member in the blend, the whole CV span of everything this
     account has ever sent is 499e-6, and four days remain. Below 25e-6 the expected blend
     movement does not justify disturbing a frozen and guarded pipeline.

DO NOT BUILD otherwise, and in particular:

  - If A fails, the number is noise however large it looks. The null arm exists exactly so
    that "+124e-6" cannot be read without a scale next to it. The smoke run at 60 trees
    produced signal +65.7e-6 against null -175.4e-6 — a perfect illustration of a signal that
    is invisible inside its own noise, and the reason C alone is not a rule.
  - If C fails but A and B pass, record it as a REAL BUT SMALL effect and move on. A real
    +10e-6 on one member four days out is a fact, not a project.
  - Do not re-run w96a with more folds, other seeds or another preset in order to move a
    number across one of these thresholds. If the rule says no, the answer is no.

WHAT "BUILD" MEANS, CONCRETELY
------------------------------
  .venv/bin/python experiments/detach.py logs_w96c_cachewin.txt \
      .venv/bin/python -u experiments/w96c_build_cachewin.py
  then the standard member runner against cache_win/, under a NEW name (`lgbm_teprior_win`).
  It writes only to `cache_win/` and to `oof/`. It touches no submission, no blend weight, no
  registration, no veto list, and nothing on the DO-NOT list.

EVALUATED IN CODE, NOT BY EYE: `experiments/w96b_gate.py` prints BUILD or NO-BUILD and the
three sub-verdicts. No human read of the table sits between the numbers and the decision.


ADDENDUM, same day, written after `logs_w96a_teprior_folds.txt` held EXACTLY ONE arm
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(`fold-0 AUC global 0.9667116527, 1025 trees` — one control number and no signal or null
number anywhere; verifiable from the log's line order and from the json, which held no
`signal_e6` key.)

THE MISTAKE THIS ADDENDUM ADMITS. w96a's docstring calls its parameter vector "the shipped
preset" and cites `run_lgbm.PRESETS["control"]`. That vector really is in run_lgbm, but the
members actually in the blend were NOT trained at it. `oof/summary_lgbm_tuned_lat.json` and
its siblings were trained at lr 0.025 / 63 leaves / max_bin 511 / min_child_samples 250 /
reg_lambda 80, passed inline as --params. PRESETS["control"] is what run_lgbm's own comment
says it is — a hand-set control vector — and it is a weaker, less regularised learner than
the tuned one. w96a is therefore priced at a STRONG operating point, not at THE shipped one.

WHY w96a IS NOT RE-RUN OR RE-SPECIFIED. The rule above was committed before any arm existed
and it is evaluated on w96a exactly as launched. Changing the operating point after seeing a
number is the move the last paragraph of THE RULE exists to forbid, and the fact that the
change would be defensible on its own merits is precisely what makes it dangerous.

THE REPLICATION, AND ITS DELIBERATE ASYMMETRY. `w96a --tuned` reruns the identical paired
design at the tuned vector. It is CONFIRMATORY ONLY:

  * It can turn a BUILD into a NO-BUILD. If the control-vector rule says BUILD and the tuned
    vector does not reproduce A and B, the effect does not survive to the learner we ship and
    there is nothing to build.
  * It can NEVER turn a NO-BUILD into a BUILD. If the rule above says no, no is the answer,
    whatever the replication shows.

That asymmetry is the whole point: it can only ever cost us a build, never buy one.
