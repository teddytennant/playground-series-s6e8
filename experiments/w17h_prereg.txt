P2. LAW-RHO is FALSE, not merely imprecise. Across a pair set of >= 60 pairs spanning
   deficit and rho as independently as they can be made to span, the ratio
   observed_sd_gap / LAW-RHO_prediction varies by more than 3x.

P3. The partner's CV DEFICIT predicts sd_gap better than (1-rho) does.
   REGISTERED: in a regression of log(sd_gap) on log(1-rho) and log(deficit), the partial
   correlation with log(deficit) exceeds that with log(1-rho) in absolute value.
   FALSIFIED IF: |partial(log(1-rho))| >= |partial(log deficit)|.

P4. There is an EXACT law and it is the AUC influence function, not rho. For scorers a,b
   on the same rows, with F0 the negative-score CDF and F1 the positive-score CDF,

     d_i = F0^a(s_i^a) - F0^b(s_i^b)            for positives
     e_j = F1^b(s_j^b) - F1^a(s_j^a)            for negatives
     Var(gap) = Var_pos(d)/n1 + Var_neg(e)/n0                                 [LAW-IF]

   computed on the pool, with n1,n0 the PUBLIC-SLICE positive/negative counts. This costs
   no simulation at all.
   REGISTERED: median over the pair set of |LAW-IF / simulated - 1| < 0.10, and it beats
   LAW-RHO's median absolute relative error by more than 5x.
   FALSIFIED IF: median relative error >= 0.10, or the 5x margin is not met.

P5. The DECISIVE CELL. Partners built to have deficit ~0 but genuine approach diversity
   (rank-averages over DISJOINT random halves of the member pool, so neither is a
   perturbation of the other) will show sd_gap far below the cross-team 53-84e-6.
   REGISTERED: for disjoint-half partners with |deficit| < 40e-6, sd_gap < 25e-6.
   FALSIFIED IF: any such pair returns sd_gap >= 25e-6.
   This is the cell that decides the audit. If sd_gap stays large when quality is matched
   and only diversity is high, then cross-team-ness IS the driver, LAW-RHO survives in
