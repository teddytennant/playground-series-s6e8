THE IDEA
--------
An OOF column built under partition P carries a per-fold CALIBRATION SIGNATURE: the rows in
P-fold k were predicted by model_k, and model_k has its own bias. So the fold means of the
column differ across P by more than sampling noise. Under a partition that is NOT P, the fold
labels are random with respect to those models and the fold means are equal up to noise.

Both our folds and (per the README) theirs are stratified on y, so the y-mix is equal across
folds by construction and cannot produce the signal. What is left is model-to-model bias,
which is what we want to detect. n is ~138k per fold, so the test has real power.

THE STATISTIC — fixed here
--------------------------
For a column c and a partition F with folds f_1..f_5 over n rows:

    z    = (c - mean(c)) / sd(c)
    S(c,F) = sum_k  n_k * (mean of z over f_k)^2          # ~ chi2(4) under "F is random wrt c"

Scale-free, no rank transform (a rank transform destroys the calibration the test reads).

THE CONTROLS ARE THE TEST — without them S is an uncalibrated number
--------------------------------------------------------------------
POS: 20 of this workspace's own OOF stems from submissions/oof_*.npy, chosen as the 20
     lexicographically first, evaluated under OUR agent/common.get_folds(y). KNOWN MATCHED.
NEG: the SAME 20 stems evaluated under StratifiedKFold(5, shuffle=True, random_state=1234).
     KNOWN MISMATCHED. Same columns both times, so the only thing varied is the partition.
TEST: the 50 pack columns under OUR get_folds(y).

PREDICTIONS
-----------
Q1 GATING (POWER). The controls separate: AUC of S as a discriminator between the 20 POS and
   the 20 NEG values is >= 0.90. If it is below 0.90 the instrument cannot tell a matched
   partition from a foreign one on data where we KNOW the answer, and nothing it says about
   the pack means anything.
   FAIL => VOID. Report no verdict on the pack. Do NOT fall back to a weaker cutoff.
Q2 RECORDED. median S over POS  >  median S over NEG.  (Direction check; Q1 is the real gate.)
Q3 THE READ, and it is registered as a three-way outcome with no discretion:
     let  t = median S over the 50 pack columns.
     t >= min(POS)                    -> MATCHED. The partition is ours.
     t <= max(NEG)                    -> FOREIGN. REFUSE the pack outright.
     otherwise (strictly between)     -> INDETERMINATE. The pack stays un-adoptable and the
                                         successor must NOT adopt it on the strength of
                                         w80a's +10.14e-6.
   ⚠ If min(POS) <= max(NEG) the two control sets overlap, Q1 has already failed, and Q3 is
     not evaluated at all.
Q4 GATING. Nothing on the send path is touched. md5 of experiments/w79a_barfill.json,
   experiments/w63a_setprice.json, experiments/w26d_queueprice.csv byte-identical.

WHAT EACH BRANCH LICENSES — fixed here so the successor cannot widen it
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
  VOID or INDETERMINATE -> nothing. The pack is not built with, not queued, not sent.
  FOREIGN               -> REFUSE. Close the thread. Record it in RESEARCH so it is not
                           reopened, the way w51 closed ext_members16.
  MATCHED               -> gate 4 is discharged BY SUBSTITUTE, and ONLY then does the pack
                           become eligible for the full vetting a build would need. Even
                           then MATCHED is not an adoption: a matched partition rules out
                           the leak, it does not establish the +10.14e-6 survives a real
                           pack refit, and it says nothing about the es-on-val clause that
                           w51 convicted ext_members16 on.

⛔ MATCHED does NOT license a send, a queue edit, or a HIJACKPRICE change on its own.
