WHY THE SWEEP IS EXACT AND CHEAP
--------------------------------
St and Sp are built entrywise from C1 = cov(A), C0 = cov(B) whose (i,j) entry depends only on
files i and j. So Sxx[js,js] for ANY subset of a larger design IS the Sxx that design would
have had on its own. One 122-file fit therefore yields every sub-design's GLS exactly, with no
refit. Q1 is the gate that proves this rather than assuming it.
