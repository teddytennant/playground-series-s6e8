THE DEFECT IN THE STANDING CLICK PRICE, stated before it is measured
--------------------------------------------------------------------
w16w_reprice.py prices the unmade click at +0.831..+3.326e-6 (limit 2) / +1.266..+4.162e-6
(limit 1). It draws pseudo-tests and reads each file's PRIVATE AUC, so E[private_k] = cv_k by
construction and WANTED (chosen on cv) wins in expectation automatically. It never conditions
on the public scores that were ACTUALLY OBSERVED. But those are the whole reason the auto-pick
is what it is: slot 1 §6 showed w16e_aonly leads blend159av_h3 by 30e-6 on public and 5.3e-6 on
CV, so ~83% of the lead is slice-specific. Public and private partition ONE test set, so a file
whose public score is inflated by partition luck must give some of it back privately. The
standing price is therefore a LOWER bound for a reason nobody has quantified.

THE INSTRUMENT
--------------
Same geometry, same seed (1616), same protocol as w16w so the first 500 draws GATE against
w16w_reprice.json exactly. Extension: read the PUBLIC slice too, then reweight the draws.

Weighting is exact rather than a kernel. Model the real world as one draw of this geometry
plus an unknown common additive gap G (the +1002e-6 CV->LB constant). Draw r is consistent with
the observed board iff there exists a G with round(P_k + G, 5) == LB_k for every conditioning
file k, i.e. G in [LB_k - P_k - 5e-6, LB_k - P_k + 5e-6). Intersect over k; the weight is the
LENGTH of that interval (flat prior on G, and it integrates the 1e-5 reporting grid instead of
approximating it). Then every private quantity is a weighted mean over draws.

TWO BRANCHES, because one assumption is not decidable from one fixed slice:
  branch GLOBAL  — one free G. Every public deviation, including the h3-vs-ens4 group gap of
      +11.4e-6, is treated as partition noise.
  branch PERFAM  — one free G per transform family (h3, ens4). Grants the family displacement
      as a real train/test effect and conditions only on WITHIN-family public deviations.
The truth is bracketed by these. Slot 1 measured the within-tight-family scatter at 7.4e-6
observed vs 9.4e-6 predicted by partition+grid alone (ratio 0.79), which supports GLOBAL for
within-family contrasts; it says nothing about the between-family constant.

PREDICTIONS, and what falsifies each
------------------------------------
P1 (gate, not a discovery). The unweighted limit-2 and limit-1 costs reproduce
    w16w_reprice.json to < 1e-12. FALSIFIED by any drift above that; downstream numbers are
    then not to be believed and the script must not write its json.

P2. The public/private coupling measured on the simulated draws — the slope of (private
    contrast deviation) on (public contrast deviation) across draws, at fixed pseudo-test —
    lands at -0.250 +/- 0.010, matching -f/(1-f) = -0.2500 and w14b's -0.2517.
    FALSIFIED outside -0.24..-0.26. AUC is not additive over a partition so this is an
    empirical claim, not an identity, and it is the load-bearing assumption of the whole file.

P3. The variance split. Writing sigma_t for the sd across draws of a contrast's FULL pseudo-test
    AUC about its pooled CV, and sigma_p for the sd of (public - full) at fixed pseudo-test,
    the shrinkage weight w = sigma_t^2/(sigma_t^2 + sigma_p^2) will be ~0.125, from row counts
    alone: sigma_t^2 ~ (1/296302)(1 - 296302/691369) = 1.93e-6 and sigma_p^2 ~ 1/59260 -
    1/296302 = 13.5e-6, ratio 7.0. FALSIFIED outside 0.10..0.15. w = f = 0.20 is the break-even
    at which conditioning on public changes nothing; w < f means the click gets MORE expensive.

P4 (the actual result this file exists to produce). Conditioning RAISES the cost of not
    clicking. Specifically the limit-1 cost for w16e_aonly rises from w16w's +1.266e-6 to
    between +2.0e-6 and +3.0e-6 under branch GLOBAL. FALSIFIED if it comes in below +1.266e-6
    (conditioning did not bite, or bit the wrong way) or above +4.0e-6.

P5. P(the auto-slot-1 file beats the CV pick privately) falls under conditioning for all three
    auto files, and stays strictly inside 0.30..0.50 — i.e. the auto-pick is disfavoured but
    NOT resolved. FALSIFIED by any of the three landing outside that band. A number below 0.30
    would be this workspace claiming a resolution the 237,042-row private slice cannot deliver
    and should be disbelieved as hard as one above 0.50.
