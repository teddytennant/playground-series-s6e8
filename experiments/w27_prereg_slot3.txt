§M1 WHAT IS UNDER TEST
----------------------
w26 §G established that the lattice cache's `CT_k` raw cell-count columns arrive
~4/3 too LARGE at serve time, because they are built from an inner 4-fold at fit
time and from the whole outer train part at serve time. The fix is a pure
serve-time rescale requiring NO refit: for a threshold tree, dividing an input
feature by s is the same function as multiplying that feature's thresholds by s.

Measured so far, ALL of it on LightGBM:
    w26l r400 fold 0   ct1.0 -> ct1.3333    +325e-6
    w26k r2000 fold 0                       +695e-6   (0.966859 -> 0.967554)
    w26k r2000 fold 1                       +519e-6   (0.967771 -> 0.968290)
    w27g r400 fold 0 control                +324.99e-6, CT_ split-gain share 1.0%
    w27g r400 fold 0 leaves31_d6            +297.11e-6, CTshare 0.8%
    w27g r400 fold 0 leaves127_d9           +401.11e-6, CTshare 1.3%

Every one of those is the SAME function class. The skew is a property of the
FEATURE MATRIX, not of LightGBM — but that claim has never been tested, and it is
the claim that licenses the expensive follow-on (§9 of the w27 slot-2 journal
entry: rebuilding all ~33 lattice members of the 188-pack with the correction).
If the effect is LightGBM-specific, that rebuild is not worth starting.

§G5(c) already asks for corrected members in three function classes. This is that,
done as a pure serve-side probe rather than as three member builds, which makes it
one fit per class instead of five.

§M2 DESIGN — exactly paired, one fit per class
----------------------------------------------
Fold 0 only, matching w27g and w26l r400 so the LightGBM control needs no refit
and is quoted from w27g.

For each class C in {catboost, xgboost}:
  1. fit ONCE on cache/f0_Xa.npy (553,095 x 184 float32), 400 rounds, no early
     stopping, fixed seed. Nothing sees f0_Xb.
  2. predict f0_Xb under four serve-side arms, all from the SAME fitted model:
       ct1.0     baseline, the matrix as it actually arrives
       ct1.3333  CT_ divided by 4/3          <- THE CORRECTION
       te        TE_ re-shrunk to the f=3/4 fit-time count
       both      TE_ re-shrunk AND CT_ divided by 4/3
     Arms 2-4 are w26l's `apply_arm` verbatim, imported, not reimplemented.
  3. record d_c = AUC(ct1.3333) - AUC(ct1.0), and the class's CT_ share of total
     feature importance (LGB split gain / XGB total_gain / CatBoost
     PredictionValuesChange), so the w27g CTshare relation can be extended.

Because arms 2-4 are re-predictions of one fitted model, d_c has NO fit noise in
it at all — it is exact for that model. The only noise is the 138,274-row valid
slice, which is COMMON to both arms and cancels almost entirely in the pair. This
is why one fold is enough for a sign.

§M3 REGISTERED PREDICTIONS — written before the run, not revisited after
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
(a) PRIMARY, the one that matters.  d_c > 0 for BOTH CatBoost and XGBoost.
    The skew is in the columns, so any model that splits on CT_ at all is
    mis-calibrated at serve time in the same direction. Registered as a SIGN
    test on two independent classes. A null or negative d_c in either class
    is the informative outcome and would say the effect is LightGBM-specific.

(b) MAGNITUDE.  Smaller than LightGBM's +325e-6 in both classes.
      CatBoost registered range  +30 to +300e-6, modal +130e-6.
      XGBoost  registered range  +80 to +400e-6, modal +250e-6.
    Reasoning: CatBoost's oblivious trees force one threshold per LEVEL across the
    whole tree, which spreads split budget away from any single column family and
    should lower its CT_ share; XGBoost is depth-wise and leaf-wise-ish in effect
    and sits closer to LightGBM. The intervals are wide on purpose — this quantity
    has never been measured outside one library.

(c) SECONDARY, and explicitly a 2-point extrapolation, so weak.  w27g's three LGB
    configs give d_c ~ 208e-6 per percentage point of CTshare with a +131e-6
    intercept. If the CTshare relation is a property of the MATRIX rather than of
    LightGBM, each class should land within +-100e-6 of
        d_c_pred = 131e-6 + 208e-6 * CTshare_pp
    ⚠ Importance measures are NOT commensurable across libraries (split gain vs
    total_gain vs PredictionValuesChange). A miss here is weak evidence about the
    relation and strong evidence about nothing. It is registered so it cannot be
    quoted selectively later.

(d) `both` >= `ct1.3333` in both classes, i.e. the TE re-shrink adds nothing or
    a little. Standing evidence (w26 §G6, w27 slot-2 §L4) says the TE arm is
    null-to-negative at pack level; this asks whether it is even positive at
    MEMBER level outside LightGBM.

§M4 WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED, AND WHAT WILL NOT BE DONE WITH THE RESULT
------------------------------------------------------------------
- No member is shipped from this. The models fitted here are 400-round fold-0
  probes and produce no OOF vector; nothing enters the pack.
- A positive result does NOT re-open the pack-level value of the correction. Two
  instruments (w27b's paired 50/50 and the 187-vs-188 cross-fitted build) already
  say `ctfix - ctraw` is a wash INSIDE a 187-member pack, and this probe cannot
  and does not speak to that. What a positive result licenses is exactly one
  thing: starting the ~33-member lattice REBUILD, which is the only version of
  the thread those two instruments have not tested.
- NO argmax. The reported number for each class is `ct1.3333` against `ct1.0`.
  The `te` and `both` arms are printed because they are free, and are read only
  against §M3(d).
- The final deadline pick does not move on this either way. Selection is on
  cross-fitted CV, per every prior prereg on file.
