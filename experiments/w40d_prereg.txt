⚠⚠ WHAT IS *NOT* CLEARED, AND THE CONSEQUENCE
----------------------------------------------
This is an AGGREGATOR. Its own log says "Loading 82 base prediction pairs ... 12 additional",
so the 94 streams are other people's, and the es-on-val status of each is UNKNOWN. Every
previous import here cleared es-on-val either by reading a log for whether it fired (w34, w36)
or by the mechanism being absent from the source (w38c). Neither is available for a pool.

Early-stopping-on-validation inflates a member's OOF without inflating its test vector. It
therefore inflates a stack's CV and NOT its LB — which is precisely the failure mode the seven
`w37_cal_*` sends queued for 08-21 exist to price, and precisely the mode that would make a CV
gain here fictitious. So, fixed here, before any number exists:

  ⛔ ARM 211 IS NOT ELIGIBLE FOR check_selection.WANTED, WHATEVER ITS CV.
     It may be built, priced, queued and SENT — a send is free and its LB reading is exactly
     what discriminates a real gain from an es-on-val artefact — but it does not become a
     deadline pick on a CV number alone. Promotion requires the es status of the nine added
     streams to be established first, on the w37 readout instrument or by source-reading.

  This is deliberately stricter than the rule ARM 202 gets. ARM 202's members were read at
  source and cleared by absence of the mechanism. These nine were not.
