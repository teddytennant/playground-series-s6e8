WHAT IT MEANS, STATED IN ADVANCE
--------------------------------
The CONTROL arm is a near-literal copy of a member the pack already holds. At r = 0.999997 the
"global TE prior" model is not merely redundant in the w24b sense (+1.07e-6, "one more member of
a family we have"); it is approximately the same predictions as `lgbm_fixed_lat`.

Consequently:

  1. d(global) is expected to be ~0, being the marginal value of a duplicate. Therefore
     Delta = d(windowed) - d(global) will be approximately d(windowed).

  2. ⚠ THE SUBTRACTION THEREFORE REMOVES LESS THAN THE PREREG ASSUMED. w97_prereg §2's whole
     argument for the difference-of-differences is that the twin absorbs the "one more redundant
     member" component so that what survives is the ENCODING. If the twin contributes ~nothing,
     there is ~nothing to absorb, and a Delta that clears G2 is consistent with EITHER
     "the windowed encoding is worth something" OR "any member at maxcorr 0.9979 rather than
     0.99999 is worth something". Those two are not separated by this design.

  3. ⟹ A PASS SHOULD BE REPORTED AS THE WEAKER CLAIM. Not "the windowed encoding pays" but
     "a member at this correlation distance pays, and the windowed encoding is how we got one".
     A FAIL is unaffected: if Delta does not clear G2 even with the control contributing
     nothing, the encoding has no case at all and the reading is if anything harsher.

  4. ⛔ THIS IS NOT A LICENCE TO RE-RUN WITH A DIFFERENT CONTROL. Building a third arm to fix
     the design after seeing the maxcorr would be choosing the comparison, and w97_prereg §5
     forbids exactly that move. With three days to the deadline it is also unaffordable: the
     two arms just built cost 5489s of wall clock between them.

  5. It is also worth recording plainly that this was FORESEEABLE and was not foreseen. w96
     chose "same model, same folds, same features, same seed, one encoding changed" as the
     control precisely to make the arms similar, and did not ask how similar the control would
     then be to the members already in the pack. A control can be too good.
