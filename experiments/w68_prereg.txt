================================================================================
2. THE QUESTION, AND WHY IT IS LOAD-BEARING
================================================================================
w65a's R-M11f asserts: "ARM 199 is the ARM 202 matrix with the three ext_members14 columns
deleted, and every transform in agent/stack.py is strictly per-column, so subsetting after
transform+scale is EXACTLY EQUAL to loading 199 members."

Reading the source, the mathematical half of that claim holds: `rankraw` is an explicit
per-j loop; `hybrid`'s `bad` mask and its `s = B[:, bad].std(0)` are per-column; `rescale`'s
`lo/hi` are `.min(0)/.max(0)`; and w65a's own `s = M.std(0)` is per-column. So the subsetted
matrix SHOULD be bitwise identical to a native 199 load, and the -5.095e-6 should not exist.

Something is wrong with one of those two statements and it matters beyond w65: R-M11f is the
load-bearing assumption of w29d_partition.py, and the 190-over-188 and 194-over-190 promotions
were both decided on that instrument. If subsetting is not exact, those decisions inherit an
uncontrolled ~5e-6 term -- the same size as the effects they were deciding.
