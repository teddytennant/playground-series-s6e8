THE HYPOTHESIS
--------------
w77d's 6.364e-6 (uncond) / 5.889e-6 (cond) discrepancy is not only curvature across an empty
hole. w63a reads its crossing across a TREATMENT BOUNDARY, and a treatment boundary is not a
place a linear interpolation may be drawn.

PREDICTIONS. Every bar below is a number, and every one can fail.
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Notation: gh0 = 1024.1576039136519 (w63a's stored gap). BAR0 = 0.9701288616800509 (live CV bar).
U = 1e-6. `Sum|w|` = L1 mass of the GLS weights w = Vg * (1' iS), which sum to 1.

P1  THE LIVE REFIT MOVES THE BAR, FOR A REASON NOTHING REGISTERED.
    Run w63a's design rule and candidate rule verbatim on the LIVE board (18/18 scored).
    P1a  |gh_live - gh0| > 20e-6.
    P1b  |bar_live - BAR0| > 2.0e-6 of CV, where bar_live is the binding (cond) crossing.
    P1c  Sum|w| at the 18-file GLS > 10.0.

P2  THE TREATMENT OFFSET IS REAL, AND IT IS MEASURED WITH A MATCHED CONTROL.
    Design = w77d's 31 names. Base arm LB0 = w63a's 8 scored. For each of the 13 fillers f,
    arm_f uses LB0 + {f} and NOTHING ELSE CHANGES. Define, in cost units,
        D(f)      = uncond_f(f) - uncond_0(f)                        own + gh channel
        C(f)      = median over g in fillers, g != f, of
                    [ uncond_f(g) - uncond_0(g) ]                    gh channel ONLY
        offset(f) = D(f) - C(f)                                      own treatment, matched
    P2a  median |offset| over the 13 fillers > 1.0e-6.
    P2b  at least 11 of the 13 offsets share one sign.
    P2c  (SECONDARY, mechanism) that sign is POSITIVE — the scored treatment makes a candidate
         look like it HURTS more. Registered on setup item 6 and I do not know which channel
         of Kmap dominates. A negative sign falsifies P2c and NOT P2a/P2b.

P3  THE MIXTURE CANNOT BE REMOVED BY MAKING THE DESIGN UNIFORM.
    ARM U = the same 31 names with LB restricted to tier1's 2 files, so EVERY candidate is
    UNSCORED. That is the only way to make the treatment uniform, because the scored
    candidates ARE the GLS.
    P3a  |gh_U - gh0| > 10e-6.
    P3b  |H_binding(ARM U) - 5.254881093054007| > 1.0e-6.
    ==> the fix cannot be "one treatment for every candidate". It has to be a constraint on
        the BRACKET.

P4  "INCLUDE THE REALISED SCORE" IS NOT A WELL-DEFINED TREATMENT.
    Over the 13 arm_f GLS fits:
    P4a  range(gh_f) > 50e-6.
    P4b  max_f |gh_f - gh0| > 20e-6.
    ==> this is the ARGUMENT owed by (a), turned into a measurement: the alternative to
        withholding is not merely different, it is unstable in the choice of which scored
        files you happen to name, and nothing in the construction picks them.

P5  Sum|w| IS THE HEALTH VARIABLE (w77 §5), RE-TESTED OUT OF SAMPLE.
    Over EVERY GLS fit this run performs (8-file base, 13 x 9-file, 2-file ARM U, 18-file live):
    P5a  corr(log Sum|w|, log|gh - mean(z)|) > +0.5. w77c measured +0.957 on a nested sweep;
         this is a different family of designs, so it is a real re-test.
    P5b  the 8-file live fit's Sum|w| (4.98) is the SMALLEST of every fit performed.

P6  THE OFFSET ACCOUNTS FOR w77d's INTERPOLATION ERROR (WEAKEST PREDICTION HERE).
    Convert the cost offset to a dCV-equivalent using the local slope of the UNSCORED curve at
    w77d's uncond bracket, slope = (uncond(lo) - uncond(hi)) / (dcv(hi) - dcv(lo)) measured in
    THIS run's base arm, and compare with the measured uncond interpolation error
    13.937812608870207 - 7.573629230554151 = 6.364183378316056 e-6.
    P6   | offset_median/slope - 6.364183 | < 3.0e-6.
    ! This assumes local linearity over 6.4e-6 of a curve that is visibly saturating. It is
      registered as the weakest of the six and a failure here does not touch P1..P5.
