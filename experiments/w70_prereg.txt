w70 PRE-REGISTRATION — CAN THE PUBLIC LEADERBOARD ADJUDICATE THE `stdcorr` CV BASIS?
Committed BEFORE experiments/w70e_basisfit.py exists. 2026-08-23, ~17:30 UTC.

§0. WHAT IS OPEN, AND WHAT THIS RUN MAY AND MAY NOT DO
------------------------------------------------------
w70 §3 established that every corrected file has two stored, defensible CVs — SHIPPED
(`fast_auc` of the shipped OOF vector) and HONEST (`base_auc + nested_delta`) — differing by
`scheme_optimism`, measured at 0.000–3.523e-6 (mean 1.771e-6) across the 20 corrected files.
Everything in the workspace ranks on SHIPPED. Whether it should is an OPEN DECISION.

⛔ w70 MAY NOT TAKE THAT DECISION: it computed the optimism table before registering any bar on
it, and switching the basis reorders the send queue and rewrites registered days. This file
registers a DIFFERENT and narrower question, blind, before the harness exists:

    Does the public leaderboard PREFER one basis over the other?

If it does, that is evidence a later run can register a bar against. If it cannot resolve the
difference, that is ALSO a result — it forces the basis decision onto the CV-comparison argument
(§3b) and forbids a later run from settling it by pointing at the LB.

§1. THE ESTIMAND
----------------
`w53a_pricer` fits public LB on (cv6, family, std, corr, era:cv6) over its sent fit table `_t`
by least squares. Refit that model TWICE, identically, changing exactly one thing: for the rows
whose stem is a corrected file, take `cv` on the SHIPPED basis (model S) or the HONEST basis
(model H). Everything else — the design matrix, the row set, MU, the family levels — is w53a's,
imported and not restated.

    D_SD  = RESID_SD(H) − RESID_SD(S)        [e-6; NEGATIVE means the honest basis predicts better]

⚠ NOTE THE DESIGN ALREADY CARRIES A `corr` DUMMY, so the MEAN optimism (1.771e-6) is absorbed by
a level term under BOTH models. What can move `D_SD` is only the WITHIN-corrected VARIATION in
optimism (0.000 on 6 files, 2.529e-6 mean on 14). This is stated here, before the reading,
because it is the reason the registered prediction below is a null.

§2. THE REGISTERED READINGS
---------------------------
P1  VALIDITY GATE. Model S must reproduce `w53a.RESID_SD` and every entry of `w53a.BETA` to
    < 1e-9. If it does not, the harness is not the pricer and NOTHING BELOW IS READABLE.
    Registered as a HARD STOP, not a warning.

P2  POWER, COMPUTED AND PRINTED BEFORE THE READING. Report (a) how many fit rows are corrected,
    (b) the sd of the optimism over exactly those rows, (c) the se of `D_SD` from a paired
    bootstrap over the fit rows, 2000 resamples, seed 70. ⚠ A null in `D_SD` is evidence of
    absence ONLY against this number.

P3  THE READING, REGISTERED BLIND AND PREDICTED TO BE A NULL:
      |D_SD| < 0.5e-6  AND  |D_SD| < 1 se   ->  **UNINFORMATIVE**. The LB cannot adjudicate the
        basis, and a later run may NOT cite it either way. THIS IS WHAT I PREDICT.
      D_SD <= −0.5e-6 AND |D_SD| >= 2 se    ->  evidence FOR the honest basis.
      D_SD >= +0.5e-6 AND |D_SD| >= 2 se    ->  evidence FOR the shipped basis.
      anything else                          ->  UNINFORMATIVE, and say which clause failed.
    ⚠ Registered as an INTERVAL with a STRICT companion, per w64's lesson 3 — a bare sign test
    on a quantity I expect to be zero would "resolve" on noise.

P4  NEGATIVE CONTROL / VACUITY. Re-run the H fit with the SAME optimism magnitudes SHUFFLED
    across the corrected rows (numpy default_rng(70), 200 shuffles). Report the distribution of
    the shuffled `D_SD`. If the true `D_SD` sits inside the bulk of the shuffled distribution,
    the true relabelling is indistinguishable from an ARBITRARY one — which is what P3's
    "uninformative" verdict means, made concrete rather than asserted.
    ⚠ THIS GUARDS THE NULL BRANCH. Per w65's lesson, name the branch: a WIDE shuffled
    distribution makes a null STRONGER here, not weaker, and would make a FIRE verdict weaker.

§3. WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THE WHOLE EXERCISE
------------------------------------------
If P1 fails, or if fewer than 5 fit rows are corrected (P2a), the design has no content and the
run must print that and stop rather than report `D_SD`.
